
## What This Crash Course Covers

### 📚 15 Comprehensive Lessons:

| # | Topic | Key Elements |
|---|-------|-------------|
| 1 | **Document Structure** | `<!DOCTYPE>`, `<html>`, `<head>`, `<body>` |
| 2 | **Meta Tags & SEO** | `<meta>`, Open Graph, Twitter Cards |
| 3 | **Headings & Paragraphs** | `<h1>`-`<h6>`, `<p>`, `<br>`, `<hr>` |
| 4 | **Text Formatting** | `<strong>`, `<em>`, `<mark>`, `<code>`, `<blockquote>` |
| 5 | **Links & Navigation** | `<a>` with href, target, mailto, tel, download |
| 6 | **Images & Figures** | `<img>`, `<figure>`, `<picture>`, lazy loading |
| 7 | **Lists** | `<ul>`, `<ol>`, `<dl>`, nested lists |
| 8 | **Tables** | `<table>`, `<thead>`, `<tbody>`, colspan/rowspan |
| 9 | **Forms - Basics** | `<input>` types, `<label>`, `<fieldset>` |
| 10 | **Forms - Advanced** | `<select>`, `<textarea>`, `<datalist>`, validation |
| 11 | **Audio & Video** | `<video>`, `<audio>`, `<source>`, `<track>` |
| 12 | **Semantic HTML** | `<header>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`, `<article>`, `<section>` |
| 13 | **Div & Span** | Generic containers, block vs inline |
| 14 | **iFrame & Embed** | YouTube, Google Maps, sandbox security |
| 15 | **Accessibility** | ARIA, alt text, skip links, `<details>` |
